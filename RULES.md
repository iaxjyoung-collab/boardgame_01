# Cold Static — Field manual

## OBJECTIVE

At the end of a round, hold 3 Broadcast Sites alone, or 4 as a formal pair with each partner holding at least one. Each winner must have announced a Signal Attempt before taking their third action that round. For a pair, either member may announce while both still have an action left. Announcing is free and public. Break a pact and both former partners cannot share a victory that round; either can still win alone.

## SETUP & ROUND

Six crews; 8 rounds; choose a 180–240 minute timer in the lobby. Each starts at its protected hideout with 4 operatives, 6 Supply, 3 Intel, and 2 private cards. Each round grants 3 Supply plus 1 per two non-hideout districts, and 2 Intel; murals grant 1 extra Supply each. Resources cap at 30. Reveal one city event. Take 3 actions each, one at a time clockwise, rotating the starting crew each round. No action clock; disconnected seats can reconnect. Host may pass a disconnected active seat after 60 seconds. The timer never interrupts a round or a battle.

## ACTIONS (one action each)

Recruit: 1 Supply per operative, 1–3 at any owned district; maximum 12 deployed, including fighters in combat.
Move: move 1 or more operatives along a connection. Entering an enemy group starts combat. Couriers may once per round cross one hostile non-hideout district to land two connections away. Allies cannot share occupied districts. Other crews’ hideouts cannot be entered.
Tag: spend 1 Supply to claim a non-hideout, non-allied district with your operatives and no enemy operatives. Underprint can once per round tag an empty district adjacent to its units instead. Buildings change hands, influence clears.
Build: spend 3 Supply for a tower or mural at an owned district (one structure per district; no replacement). Rain adds 1; Rust discounts its first build by 1.
Spread: each mural acts once per round for 1 Supply, adding one influence to an adjacent unoccupied, non-allied, non-hideout district. Choir may once per round select two different adjacent targets for that same cost. At two influence markers, claim the district immediately; its structure transfers. Enemy presence blocks spreading.
Gather: gain 2 Supply and 1 Intel (Quiet channels adds 1 Intel).
Explore: costs 1 Intel; draw one private Operation card if below the limit of 3. First entry into a street district also reveals a cache: 2 Supply, 1 Intel, or one card.
Scan: costs 1 Intel; choose your tower and an enemy with units in its range, inspect one random card and its current hand count. Normal range 1; Glass range 2. Reports are private historical observations, not live tracking.
Pass: take no operation.

## COMBAT

Both fighters privately lock 0–3 Supply and optionally one card. Neither choice is revealed until both lock; choices cannot change. Strength = operatives + Supply + card bonus. Defender wins ties. Counterfrequency cancels only the opponent’s card bonus (two Counterfrequencies cancel each other). Both spend their Supply and discard their played card. Loser loses 1 operative unless Ghost route prevents it; survivors retreat automatically to the nearest owned district (shortest graph distance, lower district ID breaks ties). A defeated attacker returns to its origin. Winners take no casualties. Attackers must later Tag to claim ownership. Structures keep their owner until tagging; hostile occupiers block their use. No combat dice. Ledger’s first positive combat spend each round returns 1 Supply; Paper army refunds first, Ledger returns only still-spent Supply.

## DIPLOMACY

Offer a pact or a trade at any time between operations; accepting transfers the specified Supply/Intel atomically. Offers expire when any operation is taken. Pacts need both players’ consent; maximum two per alliance. Break a pact free of actions for 2 Intel, even just before your attack. Your former partner gains 2 Supply and both of you cannot share a victory that round, including by fallback score. Solo victory remains possible. Promises are unenforceable. You cannot attack, tag, spread into, or scan your ally.

## SURVEILLANCE & SECRECY

Operatives and territory are public. Only owners receive their cards and scan reports. Your towers passively expose the current hand count of opponents with operatives within range; use Scan to inspect a random card. The log never discloses unrevealed commitments or private draws. Hideouts cannot be captured; recruit there to recover. No elimination.

## WINNING & TIME LIMIT

Check announced Signal Attempts after all 18 actions. Among qualifying solo/pair contenders, most sites wins, then most districts. If still tied, play the next round. At round 8 or after the timer expires, finish the current round: check Signal first, then fallback. Fallback: 2 points/site + 1/other district; allied crews combine scores minus 3. An alliance containing a crew barred by betrayal this round is ineligible; a solo crew remains eligible. Highest score wins; tied contenders share victory. If no contender is eligible, no one secures the city: a deadlock ending.

## ORDER & RECONNECT

Server arrival order resolves public actions and offer acceptance. Combat locks may arrive in either order; resolution waits for both and applies once. An operation consumes its turn before any other operation can run. A Signal announcement can be made during combat. Alliances and trades wait until combat ends. If the server restarts, saved state, timer and private reconnect seats survive. Keep your browser storage; reconnect tokens are private. Host can reset to the lobby; all six players keep their seats and factions.

## FACTION CARDS

**The Underprint** — Once per round, tag an adjacent empty district without moving there.

**Glass Ministry** — Your towers can inspect opponents within two connections instead of one.

**Rust Union** — Your first tower or mural each round costs 1 less Supply.

**Night Couriers** — Once per round, move through one hostile district to a district beyond it.

**Choir Zero** — Once per round, one mural spreads influence to two different adjacent districts.

**The Red Ledger** — Once per round, reclaim 1 Supply from a revealed combat commitment.

## OPERATION CARDS

**Signal surge** — +2 combat strength.

**Counterfrequency** — +1 strength; cancel the opponent’s card strength bonus.

**Ghost route** — If you lose, prevent your one operative casualty.

**Paper army** — After combat, refund up to 2 of your committed Supply.

**False intelligence** — +3 strength if you commit 0 Supply; otherwise +0.
